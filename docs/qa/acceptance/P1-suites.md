Both runs used `solus_test_d7fa72c2` for about 38 s, so the gate's run may show collision noise from that window. That A2 result (`68 passed in 34.06s`) is discarded. A2 was re-run after the gate's pytest exited at 21:46:02, and every later launch aborts when the pre-check finds a pytest.
- `make gen-openapi` writes `json.dumps(indent=2)` and raw `openapi-typescript` output. The committed files are prettier-formatted, so drift is judged after `npx prettier --write`, as the regeneration recipe prescribes.
- The 16 `f5042483` failures are main's own and are not fixed here. They need a follow-up on main: `ScriptedModel.stream(..., show_thinking=...)` in the acceptance module, and `thinking_chars` in `test_turn_budget`'s expected event keys.
